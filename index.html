    Limits of the automatic updates

    • UK and US are read from official data feeds, so their status headline and level update by themselves.
    • France, Italy and Germany are only watched for changes. When their page wording changes, a "Page changed" entry quotes the new text in its original language. The status headline, such as France's colour or Germany's warning, is still updated by hand.
    • Italy's page may need a browser to load. If a check fails, that card shows "Manual" instead of "Live".
    • A card says "Live" only if its last successful check was within the past day.
    • Summaries written by hand are in English and may differ from the official wording. The map is schematic, with approximate outlines and distances.
    • Always open the official link on each card before you quote anything.
